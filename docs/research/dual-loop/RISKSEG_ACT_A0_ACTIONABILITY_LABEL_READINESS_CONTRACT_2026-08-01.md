# RISKSEG-ACT A0: Actionability Label Readiness Audit

## Decision

The next stage is label-mechanism and observability auditing, not model
training. `RISKSEG-ACT A0` asks only:

> Under a declared visual information condition, can two isolated reviewers
> stably label route intrusion, boundary relation, alertability, passed-clear
> state, and epistemic abstention?

The consumed 30-event cohort is a label-design and failure-analysis set. It is
not restored to fresh or held-out status and cannot promote a model or App.

The 120-anchor pass is a fail-fast screen, not a replacement for the requested
frame-by-frame annotation. If the screen reaches a current-only or causal
observation pass, A0 continues to a full-frame stage: all 1,920 frames are
reviewed under `CURRENT_ONLY`; causal and hindsight conditions cover every
frame with their complete frozen context, while context-incomplete edge frames
remain explicitly not evaluable. Anchor interpolation is inspection-only and
cannot satisfy this requirement. Only a full-frame A0 pass may authorize the
new cohort contract.

## A new construct, not renamed central obstruction

A0 does not reuse `VISIBLE_CENTRAL_OBSTRUCTION`, `STABLE_PRESENT`, central
line-of-sight occupancy, or the closed D0-A prompt. That fresh route reached
only `16/24` observation agreement and remains
`CENTRAL_OBSTRUCTION_AUXILIARY_FEATURE_ONLY`.

The new primitive is the relation between visible evidence and a frozen
image-space candidate path corridor. Entity presence alone is not positive.
Parallel/lateral evidence can remain semantically boundary-like while being
non-intruding. This is still an image-space proxy, not a physical collision
envelope or safety truth.

## Inputs and the left-censoring ceiling

The bound input contains 30 parent events from 30 source sessions and 1,920 RGB
frames. Four content-blind anchors per event are generated by:

```text
t_k = round_half_up(10 + k * (N - 21) / 3), k = 0..3
```

Every anchor therefore has ten preceding and ten following frames. Anchors are
repeated observations; the parent natural event/source session is the
independent unit.

This cohort cannot complete onset readiness: 14 of 16 positives already begin
inside the old alertable interval at frame 0. A0 may learn whether fixed
observations are labelable, but no outcome on this cohort can establish true
alert onset, adequate warning lead time, or training authority. An observation
pass ends with `...ONSET_COHORT_REQUIRED`.

## Six isolated passes

Each condition has two fresh reviewers, and no reviewer may see the same item
under another condition:

1. `CURRENT_ONLY`: frame `t` only;
2. `CAUSAL_HISTORY`: `[t-10, ..., t]`, no future;
3. `HINDSIGHT_REFERENCE`: `[t-10, ..., t+10]`, reference-only.

Each pass receives independently permuted, opaque item IDs. Reviewer packets
must not expose event/session identity, bucket, positive status, old intervals,
oracle masks, truth ledgers, model outputs, P0 scores, or another review. There
is no third-Agent adjudication. Disagreement remains disagreement.

## Orthogonal labels

Every anchor includes six fixed corridor cells (far/near × left/center/right).
Each cell is `INTRUDING`, `NON_INTRUDING`, or `UNKNOWN`. Boundary relation is
one of `TRANSVERSE_CROSSING`, `PARALLEL_BOUNDARY`, `AMBIGUOUS`, or
`NOT_APPLICABLE`. Alertable and passed are independent three-state fields
(`YES/NO/UNKNOWN`), accompanied by `KNOWN/UNKNOWN` and an optional hazard
family.

Agent `UNKNOWN` is epistemic abstention. It is not the old
`unknown_nonwalkable` semantic class and must never be folded into
non-actionable or safe.

The program derives:

```text
ACTIONABLE_NOW
NON_ACTIONABLE_NOW
ABSTAIN_NOT_EVALUABLE
```

Any required unknown field produces abstention. `ACTIONABLE_NOW` requires
known evidence, `alertable=YES`, `passed=NO`, and at least one intruding cell.
Every non-actionable label must retain a reason such as lateral/parallel,
too-early, passed-clear, clear-forward, or no hazard in scope.

`PRE_ALERT` is not a causal A0 target because it usually means “will become
alertable later.” It may be a hindsight diagnostic only and cannot directly
supervise current-only or causal-history training.

## Gates

Primary agreement is computed on the 120 raw anchors and summarized by parent
event. Deterministically expanded per-frame rows are inspection aids only and
never inflate the denominator.

Current and causal passes require:

- alertable, passed, and knownness exact agreement `>=0.85`;
- boundary-relation exact agreement `>=0.85`;
- intrusion spatial-unit F1 `>=0.80`;
- derived exact agreement `>=0.80`, macro Jaccard `>=0.70`;
- actionable-union agreement `>=0.75`;
- parent-event four-anchor sequence match `>=0.80`;
- union abstain burden `<=0.30`;
- every hidden source stratum derived agreement `>=0.60`;
- `UNKNOWN -> NON_ACTIONABLE` violations exactly `0`.

Hindsight is reference-only and uses stricter `0.90` exact/sequence gates and
`0.85` intrusion F1. Zero denominators fail closed.

Possible terminals:

- `A0_CURRENT_OBSERVATION_LABELS_READY_BUT_ONSET_COHORT_REQUIRED`;
- `A0_CAUSAL_OBSERVATION_LABELS_READY_BUT_ONSET_COHORT_REQUIRED`;
- `STOP_RUNTIME_LABEL_NOT_RELIABLE_HINDSIGHT_ONLY`;
- `STOP_ACTIONABILITY_RUNTIME_LABEL_PROTOCOL_NOT_RELIABLE`;
- `STOP_ACTIONABILITY_REFERENCE_CONSTRUCT_UNSTABLE`;
- `HOLD_ACT_A0_CONTRACT_NOT_READY`.

No A0 terminal authorizes training. An observation pass authorizes only a new
stage in the frozen sequence: the fixed-anchor pass authorizes full-frame A0
review; only a full-frame pass authorizes a matched-pair cohort contract with
onset-complete positive windows.

## Future model boundary

If a later fresh cohort passes label readiness, a lightweight shared encoder may
use separate intrusion, boundary-relation, alertable, passed, knownness, and
semantic-auxiliary heads. A minimized total loss must add nonnegative weighted
losses:

```text
L = lambda_i L_intrusion
  + lambda_b L_boundary_relation
  + lambda_a L_alertable
  + lambda_p L_passed
  + lambda_k L_knownness
  + lambda_s L_semantic_aux
```

The minus signs in the initial proposal would be incorrect for minimized
losses. Abstained actionability items are masked from positive/negative loss and
handled by the knownness head. Hindsight reference labels cannot leak into a
runtime head.

Until a new onset-complete, session-disjoint cohort exists:

```text
NO_MODEL_TRAINING
KEEP_YOLO_BASELINE
DEFAULT_APP_UNCHANGED
```
