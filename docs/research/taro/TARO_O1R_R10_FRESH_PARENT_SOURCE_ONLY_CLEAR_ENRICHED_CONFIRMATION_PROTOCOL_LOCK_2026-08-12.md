# TARO O1R R10 fresh clear-enriched confirmation protocol

R10 freezes 32 new ARKitScenes Training visits before network or source access.
The R9 selector is applied only after source-only Phase A; all 32 scores and the
top eight must be sealed before the first FARO read.

Phase B requires 8 evaluable parents, at least 100 occupied labels across six
parents, and at least 12 clear labels across four parents. Twelve error-free
clear outcomes give a one-sided 95% Wilson specificity lower bound of 0.816,
above the fixed 0.8 guardrail. The old 50-query R7/R8 terminals remain unchanged.

Unselected FARO, post-outcome reselection, training, and treating `UNKNOWN` as a
negative are forbidden. A pass is WILD_LAB evidence for the positive-occupancy
factor and negative control only; it does not authorize clear output or any
deployment, product, or safety claim.
