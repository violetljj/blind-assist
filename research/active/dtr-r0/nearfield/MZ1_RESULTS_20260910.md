2026-09-10 EXPLORE. [Frozen protocol](MZ1_PROTOCOL_20260910.md).

## What was actually trained

The RGB backbone, original B, JOINT decoder, normalization and original alert
thresholds stayed frozen. A forward hook cached the normalized12x64 decoder
input plus four range probabilities (772 features). Clean64-zone two-surface
ranges/4 plus validity supply256 features. Native labels are targets only.

All three arms share the same132228-parameter1028->128->4 MLP, initialization
seed53, seed59 TRAIN_ONLY sampling and AdamW/BCE settings. Input ablation zeros
the other modality. Exactly300 steps per arm; no augmentation, early selection,
seed sweep or EVAL training. Spatial predictions are independent of retained B
alerts; the hard category gate is not imposed on learned outputs.
