# City 50-group acquisition pilot

User-requested small-batch engineering capture, not model training or a held-out
algorithm experiment. Fifty groups mean 150 frames: clear, center placement and
right-side placement at the same camera pose in every group.

The deterministic design combines five existing meshes (bicycle, scaffold frame,
barricade, picnic table and park bench), two existing regions of Street200V7,
and five settings per asset/region. The settings pair front-bound anchors
0.9/1.5/2.3/3.5/6 m with yaw 0/45/90/135/180 degrees, and move the camera along
world X by -2/-1/0/1/2 m. Distance, yaw and camera shift co-vary: this is coverage
sampling, not an independent factorial study. Both regions belong to one map.

Every group has its own clear baseline, group ID and three variant IDs. Real
asset bounds control placement only; native visible depth supplies BODY/HEAD
support and distance evidence. A centered object need not yield positive 3 m
support, especially for the farther settings. A side variant or zero support
does not certify free space. Existing 1/4/8 m distance policy is experimental.

Reproduce with `tools/make_city_obstacle_suite.py --trial-50-groups`, providing
the verified V7 build and a fresh output path. Then use the existing native
capture launcher and world verifier. The prior 24- and 26-frame modes remain
available. The first pilot retained 120 settling ticks and a 50 ms interval.
The user requested a speed correction, so that run was deliberately stopped;
its partial payloads and cancellation/release receipts remain retained.

The fast profile uses 32 settling ticks with no fixed delay. Native asset,
shader and streaming readiness gates and full RGB/depth/appearance exports
remain enabled. Redundant dependency export is disabled. The old settling
defaults remain unchanged for other specs. No online augmentation is applied.

the corresponding retained slow frames. Native geometry passes. RGB mean
absolute differences are 0.625–1.411 on the 0–255 scale; fractions of depth

and depth-change fraction <=0.1%. Fast capture-script time is 13.375 s and

the cancelled slow batch has no full acceptance or matched full-run speed.

## Completed fast batch

All 50 groups / 150 frames pass capture, source integrity, native geometry,
and grouped payload checks. Capture-script time is 150.266 s; process lifecycle
is 185.219 s (3 min 5 s). Geometry verification takes 4.640 s and group summary
2.442 s. This is the actual warm-project pilot throughput, not a cold-machine
or sustained multi-hour benchmark. Task processes are released; caches remain.

`world-verification.json` and `group-summary.json` preserve the full results.
All 50 clear and 50 side variants have no observed relevant support. Among
50 centered variants, the 3 m support pairs are BODY-only 24, BODY+HEAD 4,
and neither 22. The separate 8 m BODY distance states are DANGER 6, WARNING 28,
OBSERVE 14, and no visible support 2. The latter two are the 90-degree scaffold
views: inspected native previews show the open passage between side posts.
The actual mesh geometry, not the scaffold category or its box, controls labels.
HEAD has WARNING 6 and OBSERVE 2, with no DANGER examples in this pilot.
This imbalance limits use as a balanced risk-state training set.

Depth-unknown fractions span 4.49–8.69%; none exceeds the summary's 50% review
flag. No near-uniform black/white frames are flagged. Inspected samples retain
scaffold crossbars, open structure and angled barricade rods. This does not
establish real-device transfer or replace a dedicated visibility assessment.
