# MZ67: add four controlled obstacle topologies

EXPLORE source coverage. MZ64 showed that learning different geometry can improve
weak-ToF obstacle events, with old false-alert costs. MZ55/MZ61 still use a small
target-family set. MZ67 asks whether open curved frames, tapered surfaces, hollow
containers and irregular chunks add native HEAD/BODY/BOTH supervision beyond
those sources. Existing city background assets provide bike stand, cone, concrete
pot and rubble prototypes. These mesh identities have been consumed as background;
they are new target topologies here, not unseen natural categories or materials.

The fixed 4,096-case spec uses four families, four relations including visible
nonintruding controls, near/far anchors, 32 joint size/yaw/tilt recipes, two site
replicas and unsupported/supported pairs. Uniform scaling preserves prototype
topology; some targets are small or raised. This is controlled installation of
geometry, not natural placement of suspended cones or pots. AABB placement and
projected size overlap express intended coverage, not native truth. All actual
labels and any intent disagreement come from the unchanged native evaluator.

Roles are fixed by joint recipe: TRAIN2,048/CALIBRATION1,024/HELDOUT_GEOMETRY1,024.
All relations, ranges, sites and support replicas of a recipe share its role.
The 1,024 exact target-geometry hashes exclude names and role labels. Keep source
roles unchanged after observing labels. Native tensor collisions, including empty
tensors separately, determine actual geometric coverage rather than metadata IDs.

Use the original frozen MZ48 capture runtime and native kernel, with exact common
mesh/map hashes on both hosts; host-local map paths may be translated only when
the map bytes match. No actor pooling, shorter settling, frame skipping, changed
render quality or target material substitution. Existing weak45-degree packets
keep finite validity, sparse/mixed/missing-return handling and explicit UNKNOWN.
Native depth, fullframe counts, role/family identity and actor metadata remain
evaluator/loss inputs only. This source does not calibrate VL53L8CX performance.

First collect the two 32-frame canary shards, which are part of the 4,096 budget.
Dense06 belongs to the worker, dense05 to the primary. The worker may start while
MZ66 uses the primary GPU; primary capture waits for actual model process exit.
Every shard has one durable attempt and owner; timeout2,400s. No main shard starts
until root accepts both canaries. Then each host takes its four unstarted main
shards,2,016 frames per host. Any later reallocation must name unstarted shards
and preserve exact input hashes. Never overlap primary UE and primary model work.

Canary admission requires all64 source/render/floor/actor-geometry checks,32 exact
paired target dictionaries and native masks/depths, at least56 collapsed relation
intent matches, every family/relation represented correctly, and positive near
and far coverage in every family. Root actually views all64 RGB/native panels
for geometry, visibility, support context and unusable cases, binding every panel.
Record original four-query labels, extra positives and UNKNOWN; do not overwrite
them with intended labels or silently discard failed rows.

Compare each distinct nonempty canary fullframe count tensor against both MZ55
TRAIN and MZ61 TRAIN, collapsing deliberate support/site replicas. At least one
positive configuration per new family must differ from every positive TRAIN
tensor in the combined references. Otherwise stop expansion as insufficient new
native geometry. All-zero comparisons are reported separately. On completion,
retain the full held-to-TRAIN collision table, family/role/query coverage,80 fixed
native audits and all2,048 pair checks. Repeated held tensors limit geometry
generalization claims; these consumed scenes are Development in all cases.

Keep raw RGB/native evidence on each capture owner. Return unchanged RGB bytes,
small observed packets, evaluator metadata and uint8 fullframe counts[45,80,4]
with valid_counts[45,80], using the established compact archive path. Validate
native provenance and compact member roundtrip. Full worker raw arrays remain
owner-local except selected evidence needed for a concrete audit. Report actual
raw/package/allocated sizes and capture/derivation/return times. MZ61's roughly
7.55GB raw and1.71GB compact for4K are planning references, not measurements of
this source. Keep source bytes; no destructive storage cleanup is authorized by
this source experiment.

Budget: at most4,096 captured frames, zero learned-model fit or prediction. A
failed64-case admission stops the remaining4,032; no adaptive geometry tuning,
replacement cases, quality retries or expanded budget. Preserve failures. An
input-identical mechanical adapter/transport repair may reuse captured data;
it cannot recapture or alter source inputs. Verify task-owned UE/Zen/process/port
and invocation release, retain evidence and report remaining owner-local payloads.
Successful admission supplies controlled source coverage only, not a model,
natural-scene, hardware, Android, default-app or safety improvement.
