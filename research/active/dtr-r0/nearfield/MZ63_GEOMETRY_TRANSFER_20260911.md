# MZ63: fixed model transfer to new obstacle geometry

EXPLORE. MZ62's matched profile-coverage change reduced accepted native
BODY_NEAR events on the older MZ55 source despite retaining many native
winning cells. The question here is whether either matched MZ62 head carries
useful sensitivity to different native obstacle shapes, and what false-alert
cost accompanies it. Existing outcomes do not establish a trained ceiling or
show that MZ61 data has improved learning: no model in this check learns from
MZ61. Source acquisition and model transfer are separate experiments.

Require MZ61's complete, SHA-bound 4096-frame source index, its ten original
archives, independent native80 audit, root visual80 review, and owner process
releases before registration or inference. The original geometry roles are
TRAIN_CANDIDATE2048, CALIBRATION1024 and HELDOUT_GEOMETRY1024; all roles are
descriptive in this zero-fit transfer. Do not change membership, exclude
intent mismatches, fit normalization, select a model, or recalibrate on any
MZ61 role. Retain every actual extra range-positive bit and UNKNOWN. Bind the
complete source-index hash in the registration input manifest; a planned
specification hash cannot stand in for the completed source index.

Freeze eight decisions and report every one: MZ37; retained OLD_NEG/UNION;
MZ56 GLOBAL candidate and retained MZ57 GLOBAL union; MZ62 CONTROL candidate
and its OLD_NEG union; MZ62 COVERAGE candidate and its OLD_NEG union. Preserve
OLD_NEG OPEN/GATED raw traces to reconstruct their union. Use exactly the
existing checkpoints and five original cutoff vectors enumerated in the
registration input manifest. Three full-image heads share the unchanged
AnchorQuery computation; OLD_NEG shares one SpatialQuery checkpoint across
two existing readouts. Strictly load every frozen parameter and buffer.

Evaluate the existing IDEAL, MERGE_CLOSE and DROP_CLOSE packet transformations
unchanged, plus a declared ALL_INVALID boundary: ranges are all zero and
validity all false, with no residual native distance sent to a model. This
fourth condition adds no RGB encoding and does not change the primary DROP
comparison. It represents total absence of valid ToF observations, not a
measured hardware failure frequency. Check its unavailable global anchor
and report every model's decisions. ST's [UM3109](https://www.st.com/content/st_com/en/technical-documents/UM3109.html)
requires checking target count and status when interpreting ranging data;
the simulation's boolean mask is not a validated emulation of that process.
Neural inputs remain
original RGB-derived features, packet ranges/validity and fixed calibration.
Family, geometry, roles, pair identity, native counts/depth and local known
masks are evaluator-only. Invalid or missing ToF and regions outside its
field are not measured clearance. These are controlled simulation stress
profiles, not calibrated VL53L8CX performance.

Reuse existing inference and saved-output functions, with the MZ61 schema
adapter and the two MZ62 frozen heads as the only new routing. Decode each
original RGB once in batches of16. Encode its trained global256x144 BOX,
224 crop and full640x360 views once each and share across packet profiles
and compatible heads. Do not substitute a common feature map for differently
trained views. No permanent dense cache, raw depth transfer, fitting,
threshold sweep or old-source replay is planned. Record actual image/view
counts, timing, backend/device and handle release, rather than estimated
speedups. Primary inference starts only after primary UE capture is released.

For each MZ62 arm separately, the fixed descriptive utility flag on DROP
HELDOUT_GEOMETRY1024 is: at least one true native-winning BODY_NEAR addition
beyond OLD_NEG/UNION, and no newly false event bit in the final OR. Also report
the same additions and all costs against retained MZ57 GLOBAL, without
switching the primary comparator or selecting a winner. Native addition
requires comparator negative, candidate positive, true/known query and a
native winning cell; an inherited final-OR hit is not a new branch success.
The strict flag does not erase useful sensitivity/false-alert tradeoffs.
Show every TP gained/lost and FP added/removed ID, including equal-net-FP
exchanges, plus raw scores and original-cutoff margins.

Report per-query TP/FP/FN/TN, UNKNOWN, exact known frames, role/family/range/
support partitions, and within-pair decision changes. Decompose candidate
winning cells into native, known-wrong and locally UNKNOWN, separately from
final accepted events. Independently reconstruct all candidates from saved
scores/support/cuts and all unions from their constituents; scalar-recount
the saved decisions. Use only admitted compact native cell counts for
attribution, after inference. Preserve prior source/model/checkpoint bytes.

Interpret transfer alongside MZ61's full tensor-collision table, both counts
and presence, whole image and each query, with all-zero cases separate.
Geometry IDs alone do not imply novel supervision. Majority repeated held
labels invalidate the corresponding novel-label generalization claim; keep
the complete descriptive source and all results. Neither a source-only audit
nor zero-fit transfer failure proves that the available information or a
fully trained representation is insufficient.

Budget: one fixed streamed pass over4096 frames and four profiles, zero
training steps, and one independent CPU score. A useful sensitivity gain
with unchanged false-alert IDs supports a bounded controlled transfer signal;
gains costing false alerts remain explicit tradeoffs with the baseline
retained. No gain leaves transfer unproved. Invalid source/input identity
is NOT_EVALUABLE. Preserve failed logs; mechanical routing repairs may reuse
identical inputs with exact parity evidence, without changing models,
cutoffs, source membership or the scientific question. Release model
processes, images and archive handles on all paths; stop this experiment
after its result, scoped delivery and cleanup. No default-App, natural-scene,
hardware or safety promotion follows from this check.
