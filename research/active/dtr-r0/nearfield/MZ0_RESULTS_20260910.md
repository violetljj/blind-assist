## What the curve does and does not establish

The curve is not monotonic: the one-zone center-point decoder discards useful
angular information and can erase correct RGB spatial predictions. Its decline
does not prove that adding a scalar sensor inherently makes an optimal fusion
method worse. This is a fixed simple-decoder comparison, not an information-
theoretic ceiling. Preserve the negative B result rather than choose a rescue
readout after evaluation.

C closes112/189 (59.26%) of A's gap to the same-FoV gated D reference. A remaining
5.13pp gap prevents claiming64 zones equal full depth. The same-FoV D prevents
conflating angular resolution with FoV. Uncropped D supplies a separate coverage
diagnostic, not the same sensing budget.

Depth-only exact accuracy is1285/1500 for C nearest/multi,1300/1500 for C median,
1455/1500 for D crop and1500/1500 for D full. Thus RGB gating adds25 correct frames
to nearest C (net), but most of the gain is geometric information. This is not
yet evidence of a new learned fusion algorithm. Full-depth labels are themselves
derived from native geometry, so D-full depth-only perfection is expected and
cannot be presented as independent predictive performance.

FoV cropping alone loses45/1500 exact frames in the depth-only oracle. The frozen
visual category gate loses68 additional exact frames at either D reference. These
are concrete reasons to test correspondence-aware fusion rather than continue
to treat the category gate or zone-center point as an optimal decoder.
