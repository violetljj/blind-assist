# HEAD-S1 support-to-decision audit

Pre-outcome EXPLORE: zero optimizer steps and no changed model. Compare current
coverage1198 and historical F1134 on their TRAIN, original DEV128, consumed
plaza750 and consumed coverage EVAL64. Keep checkpoints separate: never splice
one model's support with another model's near score. Existing cached predictions
supply every partition except coverage1198/plaza, which receives one frozen
batch32 forward pass only if no matching cache exists. No Dice rerun is needed.

Four fixed scores per BODY/HEAD: current near probability, maximum support
probability, top6 mean support probability, and logsumexp of reconstructed support
logits. Use all576 cells, never a ground-truth mask or positive ROI. k=6 is fixed
before outcomes, motivated by the small support areas already observed; no k or
temperature sweep. The cache stores sigmoid probabilities, so the LSE is an
inverse-sigmoid reconstruction, not original raw logits. Record clipping/0/1
saturation; sigmoid(LSE) is a monotone representation for the ranking evaluator.

Report AUC/AP and descriptive attainable recall at empirical FPR<=1/5/10%,
with sample counts. These label-derived envelopes are not transferred operating
points and do not set or change deployment/model thresholds. No new calibration,
training, capture, model selection or MIL/consistency implementation follows
automatically. All evaluation sets here are consumed Development evidence.

A stronger support-derived ranking would show useful information surviving the
support branch that the current near score does not exploit. It would not isolate
absolute-position weights, gate magnitude or causality. Failure of these simple
aggregates also cannot exclude a better learned readout, since maxima/top-k/LSE
discard feature content and may respond to background. Use this small audit to
prioritize the next hypothesis, not force an exhaustive readout-versus-backbone
dichotomy. Preserve UNKNOWN as unknown in labels; support score aggregation must
not use evaluator-only knowledge to suppress unknown image locations.

## Outcome: no HEAD rescue by simple support existence scores

The current coverage1198 model does not show the proposed large support-readout
advantage. On DEV its near HEAD AUC0.930 beats max/top6/LSE0.795/0.808/0.807;
on coverage EVAL near0.709 beats0.651/0.648/0.647. On plaza, support AUC rises
relative to the very weak near score but all remain below0.5, and every score's
attainable recall at FPR<=1/5/10% is0. The support branch's scalar existence
scores do not carry an obvious usable HEAD signal that the near head alone
has discarded.

On coverage EVAL, max has a small descriptive R@10% advantage13/32 over near

derived operating point is not evidence for a robust replacement. No threshold
is transferred, adjusted or chosen for the model from this comparison.

These results do not support immediately replacing Flatten+Linear with a simple
support-only global maximum/top-k/LSE. They favor investigating stability of
cross-region local evidence before assuming the near readout is the dominant
failure. They do not prove the readout is harmless, that a shared learned
feature scorer cannot help, or that appearance is the sole cause. These four
scores discard different information; neither outcome uniquely identifies
causality. No absolute-position intervention was performed.

A next separately authorized paired-appearance experiment would need actual
geometric/visibility truth checks: changing backgrounds or lighting must not
silently alter visibility/support/UNKNOWN. Identical actor coordinates alone
are insufficient. This audit does not start consistency training, collect new
scenes, change the model, or promote any score.
