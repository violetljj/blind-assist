# MZ2: coarse depth retains most of the measured fusion gain

2026-09-10 EXPLORE. [Frozen protocol](MZ2_PROTOCOL_20260910.md).
Matched 4x4 and 8x8 fits differ by one exact EVAL frame. This run does not
establish that 64 zones are necessary, or that increasing resolution improves
every error count. Preserve MZ0 as the retained component and MZ1 as a challenger.

Each new coarse arm uses the same MZ1 initialization, TRAIN_ONLY schedule,
architecture, optimizer and exactly 300 steps. Raw native pixels are aggregated
at the declared resolution; coarse packets are replicated to the common input
shape without adding information. DEV exact is 858/1000, 868/1000 and 878/1000
for 1x1, 4x4 and 8x8 respectively. No split selects a winner or another fit.
Unlike MZ0's fixed center-ray scalar decoder, learned scalar fusion beats the
matched RGB control here. The earlier scalar failure was decoder-dependent.

## Separate frozen 8x8 corruption stress

| Fixed checkpoint input | Exact /1500 | Wrong-far /600 | Combined cross-body /600 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Clean |1353 (90.20%)|12|19|
| Gaussian range noise, sigma 0.05 m |1354 (90.27%)|13|19|
| 20% whole-zone dropout |1340 (89.33%)|11|15|
| Right shift one column, empty column invalid |1311 (87.40%)|7|19|

These are three fixed single-factor interventions, not matched retraining.
The one-frame noise increase is not evidence that noise helps. Packet shift
loses 42 exact frames (2.80 percentage points), supporting attention to spatial
correspondence. It is a coarse alignment stress, not calibrated hardware error.
No temporal fusion or walking-jitter benefit is measured by MZ2.

## Evidence and disposition

Original alerts remain unchanged on all 5000 frames. One native pass, two
300-step fits and three fixed stress evaluations took 50.98 seconds on CUDA.
Artifacts are under `artifacts.local/work/mz2-resolution-20260910/`: cache-v1
holds observations and hashes; run-v1 holds checkpoints, predictions, metrics,
receipts, stress inputs and independent-audit.json. Independent NumPy scoring
matches every split and stress result. Checkpoint replay has zero maximum logit
error; four native samples match independently aggregated packets; all 1500
EVAL scalar packets match MZ0. Initial schedule and stress seeds are verified.

This completes the declared MZ2 budget. The result is an information-resolution
diagnostic on consumed controlled Development frames, not a hardware test,
fresh natural generalization, or a promotion of the MZ1 challenger.
