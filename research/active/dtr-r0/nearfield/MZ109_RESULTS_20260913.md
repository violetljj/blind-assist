## Mechanism and interpretation

Frozen MZ108 image regions and reciprocal associations enter the interval module.
It conservatively bounds pinhole projection with horizontal range+/-0.15m,
yaw+/-(0.5+0.2*time_s)deg,pitch+/-0.5deg and image-box edges+/-2px.
Analytic interval arithmetic includes trigonometric interior extrema; it does
not infer certainty from sampled parameter endpoints. OUT requires the whole
outer envelope to miss the corridor; IN requires a fixed image witness whose
whole projection envelope lies inside it. Other cases remain AMBIGUOUS.

These conclusions are conditional on a filled-box/radial-shell representation,
correct association and stated working envelopes. The tolerances are not
calibrated confidence intervals, and a real obstacle need not fill a bounding
box. No conclusion proves real-world clearance. Independent ToF and unassociated
Radar support are preserved. Ambiguity restores the original per-return baseline
support, while aggregate absence stays UNKNOWN.
