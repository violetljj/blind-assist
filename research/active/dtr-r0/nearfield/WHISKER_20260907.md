# NF-G8: RGB-only current near obstruction and closing

Phase: `EXPLORE`, completed controlled synthetic Development.
Disposition: task-supervised current-near learning and temporal closing are useful
components in this source; bio is a recall/FPR tradeoff, not an unconditional
replacement. Historical DTR/App authority is unchanged.

## Question and change

Can task-supervised RGB learning keep a visible body/head obstacle after the wearer
stops, while separating closing from static presence and camera rotation?
[G7](CONTACT_RETINA_20260907.md) found useful learning signal but no overall
structural replacement gain. It supplied ideal metric poses, had only24 parameter
groups, and conflated near presence with conditional contact horizons. G8 changes
the task, source and available information; G7's consumed scores stay frozen.

Two near outputs query the fixed forward BODY and HEAD corridors, out to3m from
their front faces. Two closing outputs ask whether a currently visible near task
obstacle is closing under the prescribed translation. This is not a TTC estimate,
future wearer-policy prediction or all-scene free-space certificate. A 2x18x32
visible-support raster supplies auxiliary task-object surface supervision. The
separate classifier is not constrained to derive its decision from that mask;
localization alone does not establish which object caused a near prediction.

All learned near/support heads see current RGB only. Temporal branches affect
closing outputs and shared training gradients; near improvements between arms
cannot be attributed to online use of past images. This explicit architectural
choice prevents zero motion from suppressing a static near obstacle. The three
arms are current-frame, ordinary three-frame learned convolution, and local
Hassenstein-Reichardt correlation with learned excitation/inhibition. The latter
is a hypothesis inspired by [task-trained LPLC2 models](https://elifesciences.org/articles/72067)
and [LPLC1 positional-motion integration](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/biorxiv/early/2021/12/13/2021.12.11.472218.full.pdf),
not a claim to reproduce an insect visual system.

## Source and information boundary

Entry: `artifacts.local/nearfield/whisker-20260907-v2/`.
The completed source contains128 parameter groups,1,024 six-frame clips,6,144 frames and4,096
three-frame windows. Groups split80 train/24 validation/24 test. Each group keeps
its near and far/lateral/above counterpart and approach/stop/static/rotation clips
together. Distance, height, size, material and camera position vary. Parameter
groups are separate; the Willow background and shape families are shared. This
does not demonstrate held-out scene/family or real-camera generalization.

RGB is640x360, learned input256x144. Sampling is simulated5Hz using settled posed
renders; wall-clock capture cadence, real exposure, motion blur and sensor noise
are not reproduced. The camera is1.70m above the floor with nominal pitch-5degrees.
Rotation clips turn the camera independently of a fixed body heading; no true yaw,
metric pose, speed, native depth or object geometry reaches the model. Nominal
calibration is validated; the neural model consumes pixels alone.

Evaluator-only box/ray intersections are checked against UE native depth within
3cm. At least three visible native pixels in a query establish its positive task
surface. Geometric overlap with insufficient visibility is UNKNOWN; a target
invisible altogether also gives UNKNOWN. Zero means no visible *task-object*
obstruction in this query; occluded/unseen space and other scene objects are not
certified safe. This is an analytic box proxy, not human-mesh contact.

At the exact stop boundary, past observations still show translation: positive
near targets have UNKNOWN closing labels at that instant. Subsequent stationary
intervals receive closing0 while near presence remains determined by geometry.
Only train/validation targets and support maps cross into the training directory.

The v1 source was stopped during acquisition after a startup sample exposed far
scaled low bars falling below the floor. Its images/logs and explicit failure
record are retained separately; no model used them. V2 raises the body-bar height
and initial-distance range to preserve visible far counterparts. This was source
repair before fitting, not a scored method retry. The frozen Willow map is never
saved or changed.

## Fixed comparison and operating points

Three seeds17/29/43 per learned arm,40 epochs, AdamW lr0.001, weight decay0.0001,
batch64. Minimum validation masked target BCE selects the checkpoint. Sparse
support supervision averages known-positive and known-negative class losses, with
weight0.25; UNKNOWN pixels have zero gradient. Architecture, loss and budget were
fixed before any fit. Single/ordinary arms have45,646 parameters; bio has48,462
(6.17% more). Actual compute is reported separately, not claimed identical.

The depth baseline reuses frozen Hypersim Small518 predicted RGB depth with
supported raw/ground geometry under the same nominal mounting. Its closing rule
fits supported nearest-distance change across three observed timestamps, above
0.1m/s. Missing evidence remains prediction UNKNOWN. Camera-only turns violate
its nominal alignment assumption; no ideal pose correction is provided.

Per-head thresholds are selected from validation negatives for at most5% known-
negative false positives; actual test FPR and UNKNOWN are reported separately.
The binary depth baseline also retains its predeclared fixed0.5 operating point;
validation thresholding can otherwise disable an entire binary-output head.
Repeated-image history uses its parent thresholds and changes images only.
Report near/closing TP,FP,FN and both truth/prediction UNKNOWN; stop/static near
retention, rotation false closing, per-clip false alerts, support IoU and pointing.
First detection is measured relative to the first evaluated positive window in
simulated time, not as real-world warning lead or end-to-end camera latency.
Shape-specific accounting keeps thin-bar results separate from panels and poles.
Matched triplets compare approach at0.4s with stop/static at1.0s, after checking
exact equality of evaluator camera, body and object geometry. Conditional stop
retention is reported only where the approach sample was detected and all three
near labels are positive.

An additional batch1 benchmark uses the unchanged first50 test windows, with10
warmups per three-seed ensemble. It includes fresh three-PNG decoding, resize,
transfer and ensemble prediction; OS caches are not flushed. This offline host
path is not camera-to-alert, phone or full user-interface latency.

A useful ordinary model may be retained even if the bio arm adds no benefit.
No recovery at a low false-positive operating point is not success. This run
does not promote an App path or establish wearer benefit/safety.

## Reproduction and lifecycle

`whisker_spec.py` creates a fresh spec. `launch_whisker.py` runs the owned UE
capture (maximum7,200s); `verify_whisker.py --stream` checks completed frames and
publishes labels only after source completion and process release. Partial source
cannot resume automatically; failure payloads are retained. `train_whisker.py`,
`predict_whisker_depth.py` and `evaluate_whisker.py` accept explicit artifact paths
and refuse output overwrite. Focused tests are `test_whisker_*.py`: source4,
model/path8, metrics7 and depth/batching7 passed in their relevant checks. Actual
CUDA training, depth inference, final evaluation and batch-1 latency also ran.
The learned-only early score and final score entries are exactly equal.

The launcher tracks its exact UE process tree and releases it in `finally`.
Images, native references, hashes, checkpoints, metrics and diagnostic logs are
durable experiment evidence, not disposable cleanup targets.

## Execution efficiency

The source reused a correctness-oriented editor/Python diagnostic path without
first estimating its6,144-frame cost. A mid-run3-second sample exported7 frames,
with GPU utilization8% at the sampled instant, UE write throughput3.29MB/s and
CPU utilization142.7% on the one-core100% convention. These are short samples,
not a stage profiler. Multiple settling renders, synchronous native-depth readback,
Python pixel marshaling and PNG export form a serial pipeline; GPU arithmetic and
disk bandwidth were not saturated in that sample. Scaling this path before a
short throughput test was an execution-planning mistake.

Future scale changes should separate fixed startup overhead from steady per-frame
cost, extrapolate acquisition/training/evaluation time and storage, and compare
the cost of optimizing against expected savings before bulk execution. This is a
short measurement, not another approval gate.

The depth stage was updated before execution accordingly: after UE release, compare
batch1 and4 on eight training-side frames, with unchanged FP32 weights, input518,
preprocessing and output interpolation. Batch4 is used only if faster and the
probe passes depth tolerance(atol1e-4m, rtol1e-5) plus exact near/ground-state
agreement. CUDA OOM falls back explicitly to batch1. Probe equivalence is scoped
to these inputs, not asserted bitwise equivalence for every later image. Record
preflight cost, actual selection and production ETA separately.

## Next-design boundary

The new proposal to derive current-near decisions from query-conditioned masks
addresses a real G8 limitation: support is presently auxiliary and the classifier
can use correlated context. Removing a bar while retaining its support box is a
useful future intervention, provided changed occlusion/shadows and visibility are
accounted for. G8 does not contain that causal test. Its fixed near/far variants
remain a controlled learning probe.

Current static/stop positions also occupy a narrower distance distribution than
approach frames. A single image can therefore exploit distance/context priors for
closing. Aggregate closing accuracy alone is not evidence of motion estimation.
The repeated-history contrast diagnoses reliance on history; a posthoc matched-geometry diagnostic below isolates motion on selected
comparable current poses, without changing thresholds or model weights.

A pretrained multiscale backbone with a shallow detail path is a candidate, not
a measured winner here. Query masks, local temporal matching and a learned
miss-oriented refinement selector have not been implemented or tested in G8.
Test whether additional local detail actually recovers misses before investing
in an adaptive selector, and compare selectors at the same measured compute cost.
The current experiment is completed under its fixed architecture and thresholds.

### Posthoc matched closing diagnostic

After fixed learned scores were available, saved-prediction diagnosis addressed
the distance-prior ambiguity using existing pose-matched triplets. This is
descriptive Development, not a new preregistered primary score. All 48 geometry
groups matched exactly; 30 BODY/HEAD triplets had closing truth [1,0,0] for
approach/stop/static. At already frozen thresholds, single-frame ensemble had
approach TP8, stop FP8, static FP8 and all-three-correct0. Ordinary and bio each
had TP30, stop/static FP0 and all-three-correct30. Repeated history yielded zero
approach TP in both video arms. Seed all-three-correct counts were single0/1/0,
ordinary30/30/30 and bio29/30/30. Current geometry is matched, not bit-identical
rendered RGB. This supports temporal input benefit on these cases without a
bio-specific gain. The script, fixed-input hashes and rows are retained as
`matched-closing-diagnostic.json` and `matched_closing_diagnostic.py`.

### Remaining avoidable baseline work

The baseline interface emits all 4,096 sample rows and consequently infers all
6,144 images, including 3,840 training images unused by validation/test scoring.
A future runner can explicitly mark unscored training predictions unavailable
and infer only the 2,304 validation/test frames plus a small training-side
preflight. This was not changed midway through the fixed run. Throughput testing
should be preceded by removing unnecessary work from the execution plan.

The [RepViT paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.09283) supports mobile-oriented CNN
design, but its reported iPhone benchmark does not measure this Android task.
[PointRend](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.08193) supports selective high-resolution
segmentation refinement; a selector trained to rescue confident missed hazards
remains our untested proposal, not an established result from that paper.
