## Structural change

C38 diagnosis separated identity continuity from collision-risk authority. Its
false positives included object-permanence carriers that had no current
measurement, declared zero-shift surface support, and were stationary or
receding. Those rows can remain in memory for later identity recovery without
continuing to authorize an immediate route-risk plan.

X78 therefore clears route risk only when every confirmed carrier is an
`OBJECT_PERMANENCE_BELIEF_HOLD` with
`ZERO_SHIFT_SURFACE_SUPPORT_CARRY`, zero contradictions, and non-closing
forward velocity under the inherited epsilon. It preserves all track rows and
identity memory. Approaching permanence carriers and every measured, metric,
or mixed carrier retain their prior authority. X78 introduces no learned or
tuned numeric threshold.
