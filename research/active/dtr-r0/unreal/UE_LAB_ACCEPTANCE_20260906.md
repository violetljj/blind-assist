remaining migration checks and sampling compatibility; it does not migrate all

## Fixed acceptance before the new comparison

- RGB-D: six ideal metric-depth views retain the 0.02 m error bound; six
  edge-target views must pass all 72 foreground/background scanlines with at
  most 2 pixels of RGB/depth edge disagreement. Verify serialized PNG/depth
  files independently, retaining failed attempts.
  A further four-frame moving target uses the live 32/0/0/0 warmup schedule,
  0.1 s settle and two RGB captures; all 48 moving scanlines retain the same
  2-pixel bound. This checks temporal alignment that static warmup cannot prove.
- Geometry: native zero-yaw posed bounds must fit the declared conservative
  pedestrian envelope; boxes must match dimensions, position and height.
  Check every new live frame and independently recompute receipt validity in
  the evaluator. Missing receipts or mismatch invalidates evaluation.
- Sampling: new scenes use 0.1 s steps so four real measurements can fit the
  unchanged retained method's 0.5 s window. Existing 0.2 s recordings and
  historical method results remain unchanged.
- Challenge coverage: four controlled pairs, eight scenarios, covering late
  crossing phase, stopping versus continuation, 12 cm obstacle versus 4 mm
  relief, and available versus obstructed side passage. All analytic straight
  controls must agree with actual trajectory evaluation. Side-passage witness
  geometry must distinguish the pair before execution.
- Research comparison: finish both existing candidate controllers on all eight
  cases (32 newly executed branches). Report contact, goal completion, delay,
  action support and changes, including failures. No tuning, extended episode
  time limits, held-out access or algorithm promotion follows from lab checks.

## Initial findings and corrections

The native 25-pose measurement found a required enclosing radius of
0.660534659 m for the current clothed model, exceeding the old 0.28 m body
proxy. New scenes explicitly use a 0.70 m conservative render-bounds envelope.
This includes a margin and empty space around limbs: contact remains a
conservative proxy, not a triangle-mesh or anatomical collision measurement.
Legacy scenarios retain their old definitions and evidence limits.

The first alignment attempt exported near-black RGB despite correct depth
rectangles: unit emission was too dim under the existing daylight exposure.
The next three attempts encountered native access violations during setup.
All failed attempts are retained. The final probe copies postprocess settings
into independent storage before destroying the old world, and uses an existing
opaque unlit engine material with a dynamic color parameter. It passed without
relaxing acceptance; the sole cause of each earlier crash was not isolated.

## Entry points

`tools/run_obstacle_research.py` now defaults live/compare commands to the
10 Hz Development bank. `calibrate` checks metric depth and alignment;
`geometry` measures actual visual bounds; `replay` retains fixed-input
perception-only regression. Historical bank runs require its manifest and
`--split regression` explicitly.

The new immutable bank is generated by `discriminating_bank.py freeze`, with
the measured geometry profile and explicit pedestrian radius. Historical
bank source and reserved split access remain untouched.
