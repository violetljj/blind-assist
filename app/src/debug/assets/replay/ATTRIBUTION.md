# Offline replay asset attribution

These four debug-only images come from the COCO 2017 validation set. The
original COCO annotations identify each image as Creative Commons Attribution
2.0. Source and Flickr URLs plus SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `manifest.json`.

They are deterministic engineering fixtures, not evidence of real-world
assistive safety. Expected BlindAssist risk labels are tied to the current
YOLO11n model and risk rules and may change when those implementations change.
